# Cold Archive Field Guide

Two guides for anyone who keeps a Shoebox Project drive, or any archive made
with Cold Archive. They are written for readers who know nothing about
computers, and they are meant to be printed and kept in the box with the
drive.

- [Ten years in a closet](ten-years-in-a-closet.md): what a drive that has
  sat unused for years can do when you plug it in, from a damp box to
  clicking noises, warnings that offer to erase it, and photos that will not
  open, and what to do about each.
- [Recovering a damaged archive](recovering-a-damaged-archive.md): how to
  check every file, rescue a struggling drive, repair what is damaged, and
  make a fresh copy, all with clicks in the Cold Archive app, plus a last
  section for a technical helper working without it.

Every archive also carries its own instructions in READ-ME-FIRST.txt, in the
top folder of the drive, and a Check-This-Archive.command that checks every
file without the app. The app is free at github.com/wwward/cold-archive.
